## Tumor-associated macrophages: potential therapeutic strategies and future prospects in cancer
[source](https://jitc.bmj.com/content/9/1/e001341)

* Tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) mainly originate from bone-marrow-derived monocytes34–36 although local proliferation has been observed in some mouse tumors
* Chemokines (eg, CCL2 (MCP-1), CCL3 (macrophage inflammatory protein (MIP)1α), CCL4 (MIP1β) and CXCL12 (stromal cell-derived factor 1α)) and colony-stimulating factor (CSF-1) are major determinants of monocyte infiltration in tumor microenvironment (TME), as well as IL-6 and IL-1β
* CCL2, produced mostly by tumor cells, which acts through CCR2 expressed on classical monocytes
* Notch mediates the expression of IL-1β and CCL-2 in tumor cells
